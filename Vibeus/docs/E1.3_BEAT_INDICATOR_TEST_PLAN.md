### Implementation Details

**Key Components:**
1. **Toggle Key:** `I` - Toggles the beat indicator on/off
2. **Global State:** 
   - `g_showBeatIndicator` (bool) - Toggle state
   - `g_beatIndicatorAlpha` (float) - Current alpha value for fade effect
3. **Beat Detection:** Uses `g_audio.levelPeak()` for beat detection
4. **Rendering:** Red circle in top-left corner (50, 50) that pulses on beats

**Beat Detection Algorithm:**
- Peak-based detection with adaptive threshold
- 250ms cooldown between beats (prevents double-triggering)
- Adaptive threshold adjusts to music volume (beatThreshold = 0.98 * old + 0.02 * peak + 0.04)
- 4-second fade-out for smooth visual effect

**Visual Design:**
- Red circle (`ImColor(1.0f, 0.0f, 0.0f, alpha)`)
- Position: (50, 50) pixels from top-left
- Radius: 20 pixels
- Alpha fades from 1.0 → 0.0 over ~250ms

## Test Cases

### TC1: Beat Indicator Toggle
**Objective:** Verify the beat indicator can be toggled on/off

**Steps:**
1. Launch Vibeus with `--debug` flag
2. Start the visualizer
3. Press `I` key
4. Observe toast message: "Beat Indicator ON"
5. Wait for music with clear beats to play
6. Observe red circle pulsing in top-left corner
7. Press `I` key again
8. Observe toast message: "Beat Indicator OFF"
9. Verify red circle no longer appears

**Expected Result:** ✅ Indicator toggles on/off correctly with toast notifications

---

### TC2: Beat Detection Accuracy
**Objective:** Verify beats are detected accurately across different music types

**Test Music Types:**
1. **EDM/Electronic** (clear kick drums at 128 BPM)
   - Expected: Indicator pulses ~2x per second (on kick drums)
   
2. **Rock/Pop** (varied beat patterns)
   - Expected: Indicator responds to snare + kick
   
3. **Classical/Acoustic** (softer dynamics)
   - Expected: Indicator responds to orchestral hits or crescendos
   
4. **Quiet ambient** (very low volume)
   - Expected: Indicator may not trigger (threshold too high)
   
5. **Very loud music** (clipping/distortion)
   - Expected: Indicator may over-trigger initially, then adapt

**Steps for each music type:**
1. Enable beat indicator (`I` key)
2. Play test music
3. Observe indicator timing vs. actual beats
4. Count pulses over 10 seconds and compare to BPM
5. Document accuracy and any false positives/negatives

**Expected Result:** ✅ Indicator pulses within ~50ms of actual beats for most music

---

### TC3: Adaptive Threshold Test
**Objective:** Verify the threshold adapts to different volume levels

**Steps:**
1. Enable beat indicator
2. Play music at 10% system volume
3. Observe indicator behavior
4. Gradually increase volume to 100%
5. Observe if indicator continues to trigger correctly

**Expected Result:** ✅ Indicator adapts to volume changes and maintains accuracy

---

### TC4: Cooldown Period
**Objective:** Verify the 250ms cooldown prevents double-triggering

**Steps:**
1. Enable beat indicator
2. Play music with rapid hi-hat patterns (16th notes at 120 BPM = ~33ms apart)
3. Observe if indicator triggers on every hi-hat (incorrect) or only main beats (correct)

**Expected Result:** ✅ Indicator only triggers once per ~250ms, filtering out rapid patterns

---

### TC5: Low FPS Handling
**Objective:** Verify beat detection works at low frame rates

**Steps:**
1. Enable beat indicator
2. Set Performance Mode to "Battery Saver" (30 FPS)
3. Play music with clear beats
4. Observe if indicator still pulses correctly

**Expected Result:** ✅ Cooldown adjusts to FPS (minimum 15 frames at low FPS)

---

### TC6: Visual Fade Behavior
**Objective:** Verify the red circle fades smoothly

**Steps:**
1. Enable beat indicator
2. Play music with 1-second gaps between beats
3. Observe the fade-out timing
4. Measure approximate fade duration (should be ~250ms)

**Expected Result:** ✅ Circle fades from full red to invisible over ~250ms

## Known Limitations

1. **Beat detection is peak-based, not frequency-based**
   - May not detect bass-only beats in complex mixes
   - Future improvement: Use `g_audio.levelBeatBass()` for isolated kick detection

2. **Indicator position is hardcoded**
   - Always appears at (50, 50)
   - Could be made configurable in future

3. **No visual customization**
   - Color, size, position, and shape are fixed
   - Could add config options in future settings

---

## Future Enhancements

1. **Use `levelBeatBass()` for kick detection**
   - More accurate for EDM/electronic music
   - Less prone to false triggers from hi-hats/cymbals

2. **Customizable appearance**
   - Color picker (red/blue/green/white)
   - Size slider (10-50 pixels)
   - Position selector (corners or center-screen)

3. **Beat sensitivity tied to config**
   - Link to `g_config.beatSensitivity` slider
   - Allow fine-tuning of threshold multiplier

4. **Visual variety**
   - Pulse entire screen border
   - Flash background briefly
   - Animate preset transitions on beat
